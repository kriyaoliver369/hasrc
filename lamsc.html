The Science of Consciousness

Operative Metaphysics for Transformation

The Licentiate of Applied Metaphysical Science (L.AMsc.) is designed for the practitioner, healer, or individual seeking to master the practical application of metaphysical principles for personal and collective transformation[cite: 2]. Rather than treating metaphysics as speculative theory, this program approaches spiritual laws as an operative, replicable science[cite: 2].

Drawing from Patriarch Herman Adrian Spruit’s foundational synthesis between Religious Science metaphysics, affirmative prayer, and Christic subtle energy dynamics, students explore the core mechanics of consciousness[cite: 2]. You will study subconscious reprogramming, the laws of mental causation, and the science of spiritual mind healing[cite: 2].

This program provides a unique framework for understanding how to consciously connect with The Source, experience unitive states of consciousness, and apply the Christic power to profoundly change well-being and assist others in achieving their highest goals[cite: 2].

For Facilitators, Teachers & Authors

Ideal for those in guiding roles who require both practical clinical competency in subconscious techniques and rigorous scholarly backing for their published works[cite: 2].

III. Applied Research Dissertation

18 Credits Required[cite: 2]

The culminating requirement of the Licentiate of Applied Metaphysical Science is the completion of a formal, original research dissertation (10,000 to 15,000 words) contributing to the practical literature of spiritual science[cite: 2].

Approved Research Avenues: Efficacy of spiritual mind healing; integration of metaphysics and modern neurobiology; esoteric energy medicine in pastoral settings[cite: 2].
Academic Review: Evaluated by the Director of Studies and designated Senior Fellows in accordance with APA 7th standards[cite: 2].
Academic Milestone

The L.AMsc. Dissertation Guidelines[cite: 2]

Unlike purely theoretical doctoral theses, the L.AMsc. dissertation is an applied work of scholarship[cite: 2]. Candidates must identify a specific problem within human development, consciousness, or healing, and demonstrate how the application of metaphysical principles provides an operative solution[cite: 2].

  • Word Count: 10,000 – 15,000 words excluding bibliography and appendices[cite: 2].
  • Citation Standards: Strict adherence to APA 7th Edition citation formatting[cite: 2].
  • Publication Eligibility: Outstanding dissertations are recommended for condensation and featured monograph publication in the Journal of Inner Spirituality (JOIS)[cite: 1, 2].

Conferral & Society Standing: Graduates are awarded the post-nominal L.AMsc. and qualify for nomination as an Associate Fellow (Assc.F.HASRC) in the Centre’s learned society[cite: 1, 2].
