Statement on Ecclesiastical Recognition[cite: 7, 51]

The Herman Adrian Spruit Research Centre (HASRC) is a learned society and spiritual education centre dedicated to the Antiochian sacramental tradition[cite: 51, 52]. Diplomas, Licentiates, and Fellowship ranks are non-secular religious awards intended for ministerial preparation, personal development, and spiritual growth[cite: 7, 10, 51]. They are not accredited by secular governmental higher education agencies (e.g., MQA, CHEA, TEQSA) and are not intended for civil employment[cite: 7, 25, 51].
