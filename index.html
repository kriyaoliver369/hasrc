Spiritual Research Hub

Researching, Preserving & Sharing Inner Christian Wisdom

The Herman Adrian Spruit Research Centre (HASRC) is a spiritual research and education hub founded in the spiritual charism of Patriarch Herman Adrian Spruit and Archbishop Frank Bugge. We serve as a vital resource hub for scholars, students, and practitioners interested in Christian mysticism, sacramental praxis, comparative spiritual traditions, and esoteric Christian philosophy.

  • Scholarly Preservation: Deep historical exploration of Independent Sacramental Movement (ISM) lineages.
  • Spiritual Engineering: Developing operative spiritual training including meditation rites and energetic liturgy.
Academic & Ethical Rigor

Our Four Foundational Best Practices

We maintain a shared-academic ministry and collaboration with credible organizations, including St. Michael's Divinity School (SMDS), the official seminary of the Australian Church of Antioch (ACOA).

1. Charism Fidelity

Focusing on practical spiritual engineering passed down through direct apostolic lines.

2. Ethical Method

Intellectually honest, non-coercive research honoring participant free will.

3. Contextual Responsibility

Bridging academic research with tangible tools for real-world personal progression.

4. Inclusive Dialogue

Engaging across academic, metaphysical, and interfaith traditions without dogma.

Academic & Ministerial Awards

The Four Licentiate Diplomas

Historically positioned between a Master’s and a Doctorate, our Licentiates are specialist religious credentials signifying advanced study and demonstrated competency.

Post-Nominal: L.S.Min

Licentiate of Sacramental Ministry

Ordination & Clergy Preparation

A practice-focused program designed to prepare candidates to serve as sacramental ministers within the Independent Sacramental Movement (ISM) and interfaith communities. Equips individuals with skills in ancient rites, altar mechanics, pastoral counseling, and metaphysical techniques to minister unconditionally.

Prerequisites: Foundational Certificate or Bachelor's equivalence
Graduation: 72 Credits • Altar Practicum • Homiletics • Capstone Project
Post-Nominal: L.AMsc.

Licentiate of Applied Metaphysical Science

Practitioners, Healers & Coaches

Designed for teachers, coaches, and therapists seeking to master the practical application of metaphysical laws for transformation. Explores spiritual science, mindset reprogramming, spiritual mind healing, connecting with The Source, and applying the Christic power.

Prerequisites: Demonstrated engagement in healing/coaching arts
Graduation: Advanced coursework • Applied Case Studies • Final Dissertation
Post-Nominal: L.Mphy.Th.

Licentiate of Metaphysical Theology

Scholars, Mystics & Writers

Intended for theologians and seekers who seek a profound academic and contemplative understanding of our charism. Explores the history of the Independent Sacramental Movement, the Antiochian lineages, comparative Christian mysticism, and esoteric biblical hermeneutics.

Prerequisites: Prior theological or liberal arts background
Graduation: Required Reading • Theological Essays • Final Research Thesis
Post-Nominal: L.C.Sp.

Licentiate in Contemporary Spirituality

Modern Seekers & Mentors

Designed for modern seekers, spiritual directors, and community leaders navigating spiritual life beyond traditional dogma. Integrates perennial mysticism, mindfulness, intuitive ethics, and subconscious dynamics to equip leaders for holistic mentoring.

Prerequisites: Open enrollment for mature adult learners
Graduation: Modular Units • Contemplative Practice Journals • Capstone Synthesis

Statement on Accreditation & Authority

The Herman Adrian Spruit Research Centre (HASRC) is a specialist spiritual research and education hub and a learned society, not a secular university. As such, our programs are not accredited by governmental bodies nor are they intended to be. Secular accreditation validates programs for public-sector employment.

Our legitimacy is rooted in our foundational spiritual charism and ecclesiastical recognition from St. Michael's Divinity School (SMDS), the official seminary of the Australian Church of Antioch (ACOA). Credentials from HASRC are valid ecclesiastical and religious qualifications for scholars, ministers, and healers.
