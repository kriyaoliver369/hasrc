Australian successor and guardian of esoteric Antiochian lineages. He expanded the charism through practical pastoral application, spiritual numerology, and deep metaphysical contemplation.

Licentiate of Sacramental Ministry

Ordination & Clergy Track

A practice-focused program preparing candidates for sacramental ministry within the Independent Sacramental Movement (ISM) and interfaith communities. Equips ministers with practical competence in ancient and liberal sacramental rites, altar presence, pastoral counseling, and esoteric liturgy.

Prerequisites: Foundational Certificate or Bachelor's equivalence
Requirements: 72 Credits + Altar Practicum & Homiletics

Structured for non-ordinands, modern contemplatives, authors, and holistic life mentors. Explores mystical Christianity, conscious living, perennial wisdom, and contemplative mindfulness without dogma, empowering learners to articulate their personal spiritual voice in everyday life.

Prerequisites: Open enrollment for mature adult learners
Requirements: Modular coursework + Capstone Synthesis Project

Statement on Ecclesiastical Legitimacy & Recognition

The Herman Adrian Spruit Research Centre (HASRC) operates as an independent learned society and religious educational centre grounded in the spiritual heritage of the Australian Church of Antioch (ACOA) and the Independent Sacramental Movement. Awards, Licentiates, and Fellowship ranks are purely ecclesiastical and spiritual in nature, designed for ministerial preparation and personal enrichment. They are not accredited by secular state higher education boards (e.g. MQA, CHEA, TEQSA) and are not intended for secular employment or civil transfer credits.
