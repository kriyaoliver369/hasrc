1911–1994 • Founding Patriarch

His Sacred Beatitude, Patriarch Herman Adrian Spruit

First Hierarch of the Catholic Apostolic Church of Antioch & a Grandfather of the Independent Sacramental Movement

Apostolic Custodianship

Consecration: 1957 (Hampton, Wadle, & Marshall)
Jurisdiction: Catholic Apostolic Church of Antioch (1959)
Charism: Meta-Catholic love, open communion, sacramental justice
Custodianship: 17 converged lines (Syro-Antiochene, Old Catholic, Anglican, Gallican, Chaldean, Coptic, etc.) + 2 Druidic/Celtic lines in 1987.

Born in the Netherlands with early exposure to Old Catholic piety, Patriarch Spruit emigrated to the United States in 1927. After fruitful pastoral years in Methodist ministry, he held leadership within Ernest Holmes' Religious Science movement, mastering the science of creative mind and affirmative healing.

Through Charles Leadbeater's The Science of the Sacraments and encounters with Archbishop Lowell Wadle, he re-discovered the sacramental altar. Consecrated to the episcopate in 1957 in lineages tracing to Archbishop Joseph René Vilatte, he founded the Catholic Apostolic Church of Antioch in 1959. Patriarch Spruit consolidated 17 distinct historic apostolic lineages, viewing multiple consecrations sub conditione as an operative mystical act of Christian convergence.

He pioneered "sacramental justice"—extending holy orders co-equally to women, including the 1974 consecration of Jennie Maiereder as one of the first female Catholic bishops in the modern movement. In sacred partnership with Matriarch +++Meri Spruit, he established a balanced leadership model. In 1987, during their historic visit to Australia, Patriarch Herman and Matriarch Meri received the ancient Druidic and Celtic successions from Archdruid Cluthin and Archmage Ryan, committing their guardianship to Archbishops Frank and Chearle Bugge.

Primate Emeritus • Over 40 Years of Apostolic Service

His Beatitude, Patriarch Frank Bugge, DD

First Hierarch of The Australian Church of Antioch (ACOA) & Primate Emeritus

Historical Office

Consecration: 1987 (By Patriarch Herman & Matriarch Meri Spruit)
Office: First Hierarch of ACOA; Primate Emeritus
Mastery: Sacramental Liturgics, Pure Numerology, Metaphysical Counseling
Seminary Legacy: Principal textbook author for St. Michael's Divinity School (SMDS)

Archbishop Frank Bugge's life bridged practical secular engineering with deep intuitive sensitivity from childhood. Drawn to church liturgy as a teenage organist, he continuously sought deeper spiritual realities beyond exoteric dogma.

In the 1970s, he connected with Rev. Fr. John King of the Liberal Catholic Church, studying pure numerology and expanding this esoteric science under Druid Archmage Ryan into a transformative pastoral counseling tool. His path culminated in extensive personal study directly under Patriarch Herman and Matriarch Meri Spruit.

In 1987, Archbishop Frank and Archbishop Chearle Bugge were consecrated by Patriarch Herman and Matriarch Meri, officially establishing the Australian Church of Antioch with 19 apostolic and druidic lines. As Presiding Archbishop for over four decades, he authored the core curricula for St. Michael's Divinity School, anchoring the Australian church in radical pastoral hospitality.

Archbishop Glenn W. Taylor's leadership embodies a synthesis of unwavering pastoral service, theological scholarship, and musical mastery across ten instruments. As founding Minister General of the Ecumenical Order of St. Francis (TOR), known in religion as Brother Paul Francis (eosf), his ministry unites contemplative Franciscan simplicity with apostolic order.

Beginning in 1972 with three decades in the Anglican Church, he transferred his Franciscan vows to Primate Frank Bugge in 2002. Ordained Deacon and Priest in 2002, he was consecrated Bishop of Tasmania in 2004, elevated to Archbishop of Tasmania in 2006, and formally installed as Presiding Archbishop and Primate of the Australian Church of Antioch on February 16, 2025.

Archbishop Taylor serves as Dean of St. Michael's Divinity School (SMDS), on the Board of Studies for St. Bonaventure College of Franciscan Studies, and as author of the ANZCA string syllabus.

Mandatory Canonical & Civil Disclosure • Article IX, Section 9.1

Ecclesiastical Standing & Operational Autonomy

The Herman Adrian Spruit Research Collegium (HASRC) is an autonomous, not-for-profit ecclesiastical learned society, research scriptorium, and educational ministry, erected by Archiepiscopal Decree as a voluntary religious sodality under the canonical jurisdiction of its Founder and Provost, operating in sacramental fellowship with the Australian Church of Antioch (ACOA).

HASRC is strictly a religious and metaphysical institute of sacred learning, not a secular university or civil college. Its curricula, certificates, Licentiates, and Fellowship ranks are exclusively ecclesiastical and non-secular in character, conferred under the inherent apostolic and canonical authority of the Provost and the Collegium for ministerial formation, spiritual progression, and theological scholarship.

HASRC does not claim, nor does it seek, secular civil governmental accreditation. Its credentials are designed for ministerial and sacred-scholarly purposes, not for secular civil employment or civil university credit transfer. Nothing taught, offered, or celebrated by the Collegium is medical, psychological, counseling, or other regulated professional advice or treatment.
