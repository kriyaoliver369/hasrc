Requirements for the Certified HOPE Practitioner (CHP) Designation:

  • 100% completion of all 12 modules and integration logs.
  • Submission of three (3) Case Studies (1 personal healing journey, 2 practice clients).
  • Video submission of the student facilitating a 45-minute 1-on-1 mock HOPE session.
