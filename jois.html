Aims & Scope

JOIS gives scholarly form to the study of the inner life of religion: contemplation, sacrament, liturgy, mysticism and spiritual formation. It does not require novelty, though original contributions are welcome. A careful reading of a source, a faithful edition of a document or a clear restatement of a tradition is publishable work.

Authors write from a stated standpoint, confessional or descriptive, and name their sources. Reviewers judge the argument by the standards of that tradition and of sound scholarship, and are not asked to adopt a laboratory method that the subject does not call for.

Article Types

Treatise / Essay

Argument-led study with its own section headings, footnotes and a short “Sources and Standpoint” paragraph. 2,000–6,000 words.

Research Article

Empirical or historical-documentary work with a clear method, evidence and discussion. 2,000–6,000 words.

Annotated Document

A historical text with transcription, notes on provenance and short commentary.

Case Study

A structured account of one community, practice or event, with its limits stated.

Monograph

A standalone book-length study of 18,000 words or more.

Publication Policies

Peer review

Editorial screening, then review of each manuscript by at least two reviewers. Reviewers are not told the author’s identity. Decisions are made by the editor.

Ethics & conflicts

Authors declare conflicts of interest and, where people are studied, ethical approval or consent. Plagiarism is checked before review.

AI disclosure

Any use of AI tools in research or writing must be stated in the manuscript. AI systems cannot be listed as authors.

Identifiers

JOIS does not assign DOIs. Authors are encouraged to deposit their PDF in an open repository such as Zenodo and cite the DOI it issues.

Submit to JOIS

Original research papers, analytical treatises, annotated historical documents and structured case studies (2,000 to 6,000 words), and standalone monographs (18,000+ words), in APA 7th Edition style.

Author Guidelines & Submission
Open Access Policy

JOIS operates a 100% Diamond Open Access model: free to read globally, no password barriers. All works are published under CC-BY-SA 4.0, and authors retain copyright.

Article Processing Charges:None
Authors’ Views

Opinions in JOIS are the authors’ own. Publication is not an endorsement by the Collegium, the Provost or any officer, and confers no rank. No article is medical, psychological, counselling or other regulated professional advice.
