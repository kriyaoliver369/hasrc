Inaugural Research Monograph

The Science of the Sacraments: Herman Adrian Spruit’s Meta-Catholic Synthesis and the Operative Mechanics of the Altar

The Rt. Rev. Mar Elisha Thoma

Director of Studies & Senior Fellow, Herman Adrian Spruit Research Centre (HASRC)

DOI: https://doi.org/10.hasrc.jois.2026.01.001
Published: October 2026
Peer Review: Editorial College

Abstract

This foundational treatise investigates the synthesis achieved by Patriarch Herman Adrian Spruit (1911–1994) within the Independent Sacramental Movement (ISM), focusing specifically on his reconciliation of traditional Catholic sacramental realism with mid-twentieth-century metaphysical mental science and clairvoyant liturgical mechanics. Drawing on Charles Webster Leadbeater’s energetic observations of the Eucharistic thought-form and Spruit’s formative engagement with Ernest Holmes’ Religious Science movement, the paper deconstructs the shift from exoteric scholastic transubstantiation to an operative "science of the sacraments." It examines how Spruit conceived the altar not merely as a site of memorial or dogmatic observance, but as a calibrated matrix for subtle energetic alchemy and spiritual engineering. The paper further evaluates the ecclesiological consequences of this meta-Catholic synthesis, notably Spruit’s historic pioneering of "sacramental justice," unconditional communion, and co-equal female apostolic succession. The findings demonstrate that Spruit’s inner Christian charism offers an integrated metaphysical framework for contemporary independent sacramental practice that bridges esoteric mysticism and valid apostolic liturgy.

Keywords: Herman Adrian Spruit, Independent Sacramental Movement (ISM), Sacramental Science, Meta-Catholicism, Altar Energetics, Christian Mysticism, Eucharistic Thought-Forms, Esoteric Liturgics

Author Biography

The Rt. Rev. Mar Elisha Thoma, Sen.F.HASRC

Mar Elisha Thoma serves as the Director of Studies and Senior Fellow at the Herman Adrian Spruit Research Centre. His research specializes in Antiochian sacramental theology, liturgical energetics, voice aesthetics in sacred acoustics, and the history of apostolic successions within the Independent Sacramental Movement.
