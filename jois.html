Aims & Scope

JOIS gives scholarly form to the study of the inner life of religion: contemplation, sacrament, liturgy, mysticism and spiritual formation. Originality is a review criterion, but novelty is not required: an article is original when it makes its own analysis of its sources, and a close reading or a faithful edition can meet that test.

Authors write from a stated standpoint, confessional or descriptive, and name their sources. Reviewers judge the argument by the standards of that tradition and of sound scholarship, and are not asked to adopt a laboratory method that the subject does not call for.

Preprints

Peer review pending

A preprint is not an article published in JOIS. It has not completed peer review and is never called peer reviewed.

Research Streams

A submission must fit at least one of the Collegium’s seven research streams.

    Article Types

    Original Research Paper

    2,000–6,000 words

    Analytical Treatise

    2,000–6,000 words

    Annotated Historical Document

    2,000–6,000 words

    Structured Case Study

    2,000–6,000 words

    Monograph

    18,000 words or more

    These are the only article types. Pulpit sermons, devotional bulletins and personal polemics are not accepted. All articles are referenced in APA 7th Edition style. Structure, front matter and back matter are set out in the Editorial Policy & Style Manual.

    Publication Policies

    This page is a summary. The binding text is the JOIS Editorial Policy & Style Manual (PDF, version 1.0, promulgated 1 November 2026), made under the Collegium’s founding statutes.

    Peer review

    The Editor-in-Chief screens each submission. At least two independent reviewers, Fellows of the Collegium or invited external scholars, then review it. Review is anonymous wherever practicable. Manual, Part C and D3.

    Status labels

    “Preprint; peer review pending” until review is complete. “Peer reviewed” only after at least two reviewers have reported and the Editor-in-Chief has accepted the article. Manual, A4 and D5.

    Ethics & conflicts

    Research ethics and the absence of plagiarism are review criteria. Authors and reviewers disclose conflicts. Where an author holds an editorial office, a Deputy Editor-in-Chief arranges the review and the article states this. Manual, B7 and D2.

    Use of AI tools

    Authors must state any use of AI tools, and an AI tool cannot be an author. The editors may use AI tools for form checks, formatting and drafting correspondence, and remain responsible for the result. A reviewer’s recommendation and the decision on a manuscript are never delegated to an AI tool. Manual, B8 and D7.

    Decisions & corrections

    The Editor-in-Chief decides, and the decision is final as to publication. Publication or refusal confers or refuses no rank. A complaint about process may be sent to the Chancery. Errors are corrected by a dated corrigendum, and plagiarism or falsification found after publication leads to retraction. Manual, D4 and D6.

    Identifiers

    Every article has a permanent PDF address and is cited by journal, volume, issue and pages. JOIS does not yet assign DOIs, so authors are encouraged to deposit a PDF in an open repository such as Zenodo and cite the DOI it issues. Manual, A5.

    Submit to JOIS

    Original research papers, analytical treatises, annotated historical documents and structured case studies (2,000 to 6,000 words), and standalone monographs (18,000+ words), in APA 7th Edition style. Send a Word (.docx) file with a short cover note naming the article type and research streams and declaring any conflict. Full instructions are in the manual.

    Open Access Policy

    JOIS operates a 100% Diamond Open Access model: free to read globally, no password barriers. All works are published under CC-BY-SA 4.0, and authors retain copyright.

    Article Processing Charges:None
    Authors’ Views

    Opinions in JOIS are the authors’ own. Publication is not an endorsement by the Collegium, the Provost or any officer, and confers no rank. No article is medical, psychological, counselling or other regulated professional advice.
